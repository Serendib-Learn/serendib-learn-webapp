SERENDIB LEARN -- BRAND TOKENS
For designers and developers. Hand this file to anyone building something on-brand.

COLOURS
Forest   #1B4332   Primary -- headers, nav, dark sections
Fern     #2D6A4F   Secondary -- supporting text and accents
Sage     #95C4AA   Accent light -- muted text on dark backgrounds
Spice    #C9873A   Accent warm -- calls to action, links, hover states
Cream    #F7F3ED   Background -- primary light background
Earth    #2C2416   Text dark -- body text on light backgrounds

Never use corporate blue. The palette should feel like a boutique Colombo
cafe, not a tech startup.

TYPOGRAPHY
Headings : Georgia (serif), fallback "Times New Roman", serif
Body/UI  : Inter (sans-serif), fallback ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif

Serif headlines carry the emotional/editorial weight. Sans-serif body text
keeps things practical and readable.

LOGO
Mark   : five-petal white Araliya flower, amber centre, on a forest-green
         circle. Files: Logo/Serendib-Icon.png
Lockup (horizontal) : icon + "serendib.learn" wordmark + "KEEPING LANGUAGES
         ALIVE" tagline, on forest-green background.
         Files: Logo/Serendib-Lockup-Horizontal.png
Lockup (vertical)   : same lockup, stacked, on cream background.
         Files: Logo/Serendib-Lockup-Vertical.png

Minimum clear space: keep the mark's own height as breathing room on every
side. The mark reads best on Cream or Forest -- avoid placing it over busy
photography.

TAGLINE
"Keeping languages alive."

VOICE
Warm, personal, editorial -- never corporate or salesy. Favour specific,
sensory, human details over abstract claims. Speaks directly to the
emotional core of the mission: "The language your child speaks with their
grandmother is worth fighting for."
